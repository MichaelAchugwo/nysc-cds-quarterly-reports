# NYSC CDS Reporting & Submission Rules

This repository maintains official Community Development Service (CDS) quarterly reports and Key Performance Indicator (KPI) documentation for the National Youth Service Corps (NYSC). All contributors and automated generators must adhere strictly to the following rules:

---

## 📌 Rule 1: Uniform & Dress Code Compliance in Photographic Evidence
* **Strict Regulation Footwear:** In accordance with NYSC bye-laws, corps members must adhere to approved uniform regulations during official CDS activities. 
* **Non-Compliant Footwear Prohibition:** White sneakers, tennis shoes, or casual slippers must **never** be visible when corps members are wearing standard 7/8 khaki trousers. (Approved footwear with khakis is official jungle boots).
* **Mandatory Cropping Rule:** If any photograph captures a corps member wearing non-regulation footwear (such as white sneakers on the right flank during the OUDA sensitization visit), **the photograph MUST be cropped to exclude the footwear prior to publication or inclusion in official reports.**
* **Preservation of Overlays:** When cropping evidence photos, GPS Map Camera metadata overlays (location, coordinates, date, and timestamp) must be preserved or re-anchored at the bottom of the cropped composition.

---

## 📌 Rule 2: Aggregated Beneficiary Reporting (No "CDS Days" Phrasing)
* **No Meeting Frequency Breakdowns:** Never mention internal meeting schedules such as *"2 CDS days"*, *"across two separate days"*, or daily quotas in public report narratives, activity descriptions, or achievements.
* **Consolidated Counts:** Always report the finalized, aggregated total number of beneficiaries reached for the month (e.g., *"A total of 40 beneficiaries (20 males and 20 females) were sensitized..."*).

---

## 📌 Rule 3: Handling Inactive Months (Recess / Off-Months)
* **Single Dash Placeholder:** For any month in which a CDS group did not hold an outreach or was in recess (e.g., August), **every cell across that table row must be marked with a single dash (`-`)**.
* **Gallery Exclusion:** Inactive months must be completely omitted from the photographic gallery annex (no blank photo cards or empty gallery pages).

---

## 📌 Rule 4: Layout & Table Pagination Integrity
* **Single-Page Quarterly Reports:** The standard Quarterly Report table must fit cleanly on **one single landscape page** (8.5" x 11" Letter landscape). Row padding, typography (Times New Roman 9pt/9.5pt), and column widths must be balanced to prevent overflow.
* **No Broken Column Headers:** Column titles must never wrap mid-word (e.g., avoiding split words like *"NUM/BER"* or squished vertical text like *"S/ /N"*).
* **Multi-Page KPI Standards:** In KPI reports, table headers must repeat cleanly on continuation pages, and signature blocks must never be stranded alone on an empty page.

---

## 📌 Rule 5: Standardized Officer Sign-Offs
* **Schedule Officer:** `OKO JOHN` (verify correct spelling; eliminate legacy typos such as `OKOU.JOHN`).
* **State Coordinator:** `OYENUGA MUJISOLA JOKE`.
* **Dating:** Dates must coincide with the end of the quarter or the final official CDS meeting of the period (e.g., `24/9/2026`).
