1. Environmental CDS Group 11 Ogoja Road, Obudu Obudu OUTREACH: Environmental sanitation and community sensitization conducted along Ogoja Road, Obudu. 11 corps members engaged in street sweeping, refuse collection, clearing roadside litter, and sensitizing shop owners, residents, and commuters on avoiding indiscriminate waste dumping and maintaining a clean roadway. 55 residents, traders, and road users (30 males and 25 females) were sensitized on environmental hygiene; the road corridor was effectively swept and cleared of refuse. Shop owners and residents appreciated the group members' sanitation efforts and committed to keeping their shop frontages clean.
2. Environmental CDS Group 11 Obudu Market, Obudu Obudu OUTREACH: Market sanitation and trader environmental hygiene sensitization conducted at Obudu Market, Obudu. 11 corps members sensitized market women, traders, and meat sellers on proper waste management, keeping market stalls and walkways clean, avoiding refuse accumulation around food items, and preventing environmental health hazards. 40 market traders and sellers (10 males and 30 females) were sensitized on market hygiene, refuse disposal, and maintaining sanitary stall surroundings. Market traders and stall owners actively engaged with corps members and pledged to uphold cleaner market environments.
3. Environmental CDS Group 3 Handmaids International Nursery and Primary School, Obudu Obudu OUTREACH: Waste management and environmental hygiene outreach conducted at Handmaids International Nursery and Primary School, Obudu. 3 corps members educated pupils and teachers on proper waste disposal, separating refuse, personal hygiene, and keeping classroom surroundings clean. The CDS group donated sanitation items including Baskets, Brooms, Packers, and Mop to the school. 35 pupils and school staff (15 males and 20 females) were educated on waste management; sanitation items (Baskets, Brooms, Packers, and Mop) were successfully donated to support school cleanliness. The pupils and school management were happy and grateful for the donated sanitation materials and demonstrated enthusiasm for maintaining a clean learning environment.
