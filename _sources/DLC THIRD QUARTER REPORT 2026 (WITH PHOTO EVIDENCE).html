1. Digital Literacy CDS Group 8 St. Joseph's Vicinity, Obudu Obudu YOUTH & STUDENT DIGITAL SKILLS OUTREACH: DLC members carried out targeted digital literacy sensitization for youth and secondary school students within the St. Joseph's community in Obudu. The session focused on empowering young learners with smartphone literacy for educational advancement. DLCs conducted one-on-one practical coaching on utilizing mobile devices for e-learning, accessing academic research materials, leveraging search engines, and using safe communication tools. Over 25 youths and students received practical hands-on guidance on using smartphones for educational research and collaborative study. Beneficiaries learned safe internet practices and academic discovery techniques. Highly engaging and well-received by community youths who expressed keen interest in leveraging digital tools for their school studies.
2. Digital Literacy CDS Group 10 Obudu Urban Dev. Authority (OUDA), Obudu Obudu INSTITUTIONAL ADVOCACY & WORKPLACE CAMPAIGN: DLC members conducted an official advocacy and awareness visit to the Obudu Urban Development Authority (OUDA). The corps members engaged public sector workers and agency staff on the national "DL4ALL: Not Just the Number but the Impact" initiative. The sensitization covered workplace digital tools, electronic document workflow, cloud storage fundamentals, and critical cybersecurity hygiene to prevent official data leaks and phishing attacks. Sensitized agency staff and civil servants on digital productivity tools and office workflow efficiency. Established collaboration with agency leadership to support community-wide digital literacy initiatives. Successful institutional engagement; agency leadership commended the NYSC DLC initiative for promoting digital readiness in the public sector.
3. Digital Literacy CDS Group 12 Obudu Main Market, Obudu Obudu MARKET TRADERS DIGITAL INCLUSION OUTREACH: Under the campaign banner "Enabling Every Nigerian Citizen to Embrace Technology with Confidence," DLC members conducted an extensive field outreach at Obudu Main Market. Deployed in peer teams, DLCs engaged traders, artisans, and transport operators directly in their shops and stalls. Members conducted practical demonstrations on mobile banking apps, USSD transactions, point-of-sale security, fraud prevention, and introduced traders to the free NITDA digital skills curriculum platform (dl4all.nitda.gov.ng). Over 35 market stalls and small business owners were trained on mobile financial transactions, secure digital payments, and avoiding mobile scams. Several traders successfully navigated digital customer communication tools on their devices. Very impactful field sensitization. Traders welcomed the practical business applications of digital tools; follow-up sessions were requested.
